# Game Concept · Project Plateau

## Selected direction

**Proof Before Dark** is a free, single-player, first-person 3D survival-adventure
slice for desktop web. In one 1–3 minute run, the player leaves Fort Challenger,
records credible evidence of a living prehistoric ecosystem, survives its response
and returns with the plates intact.

The adaptation converts the novel's proof problem into play: a sighting is not proof
until the record survives the return. Chapters 4, 10–12 and 16 establish the disputed
expedition, observation sites, wildlife danger, damaged evidence and final public proof.

```text
leave safety → read terrain and wildlife → expose a plate → survive the response
→ choose a return route → extract with credible, partial or lost proof
```

The largest unknown is whether photography feels like a physical risk rather than a
journal interaction. The prototype exists to answer that question.

## Locked product frame

- English-first international audience; no knowledge of the novel required.
- Desktop WebGL2, keyboard and mouse, connected first-person 3D spaces.
- Teen-equivalent peril, no gore, backend, monetization or multiplayer.
- Familiar traversal, observation, stealth and limited-defense verbs.
- One bounded run, not an open world, crafting game, arena shooter or chapter reader.

The primary gameplay precedent is *Subnautica*: wonder and danger occupy the same
exploration route, and observation produces useful knowledge. The adaptation does not
borrow its fiction, art, crafting, vehicles or interface. *The Long Dark* and
*Alien: Isolation* are auxiliary references for landmark navigation and a threat that
must be outread rather than cleared.

## Three directions considered

| Direction | Source slice and dominant action | Distinct pressure | Main risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A · Proof Before Dark** | Field observation and damaged-proof return across Chapters 10–12 and 16 | Better evidence requires exposure; recording changes the safe route | Photography could become a progress bar |
| **B · Fire Across the Lake** | Malone follows the brook to the lake and escapes a tracking predator in Chapter 12 | Sound guides navigation while time and pursuit close the return window | Darkness could hide the colourful expedition identity |
| **C · The Eighteenth Cave** | Survey, missed fork and proof-carrying descent in Chapters 15–16 | The obvious lit route is wrong; cargo makes recovery costly | Cave navigation could read as a literary puzzle rather than survival play |

All three support repeatable player decisions and bounded prototypes. Direction A wins
because it carries the whole adaptation promise—scout, document, survive and extract—
in one readable daylight frame, while B narrows the game to pursuit and C narrows it to
route interpretation.

## Selected experience contract

### Player identity and verbs

The player is the expedition's forward scout. They traverse, inspect, frame, record,
use cover, evade, spend a scarce defensive shot, choose a route and extract. Defense
creates distance; killing or clearing wildlife is never the optimal loop.

### Three-part action arc

1. **Explore:** follow water, tracks, calls and landmarks from Fort to a covered blind.
2. **Develop:** choose an observation position and hold a live frame long enough to
   record scale or behaviour.
3. **Mature:** read the ecosystem's response, combine cover or limited defense with a
   return-route choice, and submit the surviving plates at Fort.

### Experience pillars

- **Read the living route:** the world communicates direction, cover and danger without
  a continuous objective arrow.
- **Proof is exposed action:** framing, position and timing visibly affect the plate and
  remain vulnerable during the return.
- **Defense buys escape:** terrain is primary; a loud weapon solves distance but creates
  a downstream cost.

### Signature playable frame

The brass-and-leather field camera lowers after capturing an iguanodon family; red
basalt anchors their scale while a pterodactyl shadow crosses the glade and the return
route remains visible. Identity, evidence, threat and escape must read in one frame.

## Prototype boundary and handoff

The slice includes Fort, brook, observation fork, glade, ecological response, two return
routes, evidence result, failure and restart. It excludes the Amazon approach, central
lake, cave people, crafting, progression, dialogue, multiple weapons and full campaign.

The selected direction is falsified if position or timing cannot create a visibly better
plate, or if recording never changes a later route or defense decision. `GAME_DESIGN.md`
must define that causal chain and preserve the non-lethal scout fantasy.
